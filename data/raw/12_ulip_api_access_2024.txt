TITLE: ULIP API Access and Logistics Digitization
SOURCE: Press Information Bureau, Government of India
URL: https://www.pib.gov.in/Pressreleaseshare.aspx?PRID=2082674&lang=2&reg=48
DATE: 2024-12-10

A December 2024 PIB reply describes ULIP as a digital gateway that enables industry stakeholders to access logistics-related datasets from government systems through API-based integration. It says ULIP operates on a request-response mechanism.

The reply reported that ULIP was integrated with 39 systems of 11 ministries, providing access to more than 1,800 data fields through 125 APIs at that point in time. It places ULIP alongside freight digitization, Multi-Modal Logistics Parks, air-cargo facilities and the Logistics Data Bank as initiatives expected to reduce logistics costs, improve transit times and improve supply-chain efficiency.

The same source says the Logistics Data Bank uses RFID technology to provide real-time tracking of India’s containerized EXIM cargo. It also notes that the Freight Greenhouse Gas Calculator compares emissions and transport costs for a fixed origin-destination pair.
