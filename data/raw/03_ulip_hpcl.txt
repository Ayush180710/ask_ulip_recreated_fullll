TITLE: HPCL Integration with ULIP
SOURCE: Press Information Bureau, Government of India
URL: https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2084910&lang=1&reg=3
DATE: 2024-12-16

HPCL signed an agreement with NICDC Logistics Data Services Ltd. to integrate APIs with the Unified Logistics Interface Platform. The HPCL API provides fuel-station and pricing visibility, including information about the location and pricing of HPCL fuel stations across India.

The PIB release explains that this kind of data can help logistics service providers, traders and transporters plan operations, improve visibility and reduce inefficiencies. The release describes ULIP as a digital gateway through which industry players can access logistics-related datasets from government systems through API-based integration.
