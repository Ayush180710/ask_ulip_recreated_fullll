TITLE: ULIP Initial Design and Integration
SOURCE: Press Information Bureau, Government of India
URL: https://www.pib.gov.in/Pressreleaseshare.aspx?PRID=1806620&lang=2&reg=48
DATE: 2022-03-16

ULIP was designed as a platform for data exchange among operators across modes of transport. At the time of this PIB release, the platform had been integrated with 24 systems of six ministries through 78 APIs, including systems associated with ports, railways, road transport and highways, civil aviation, foreign trade and customs.

The release describes the objective as enabling access to data available with government systems for logistics and supporting more efficient movement of goods.
