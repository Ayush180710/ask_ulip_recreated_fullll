TITLE: National Logistics Policy 2022 — Summary
SOURCE: Department for Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade / Government of India
URL: https://www.dpiit.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/07/08314f7bc6b0366f257fe85e0c40b3e6.pdf
DATE: 2022-09-28

The National Logistics Policy 2022 was notified for implementation in September 2022. The policy explains that logistics efficiency depends on infrastructure, services such as digital systems, processes and regulatory frameworks, and human resources.

The policy distinguishes the role of PM GatiShakti National Master Plan and the National Logistics Policy. PM GatiShakti focuses on integrated infrastructure and network planning for multimodal connectivity, while the National Logistics Policy addresses efficiency in services, processes, digital systems, regulatory frameworks and human resources.

The policy seeks an integrated, technologically enabled, cost-efficient, resilient, sustainable and trusted logistics ecosystem. It is intended to support smoother movement of goods and improve logistics performance.
