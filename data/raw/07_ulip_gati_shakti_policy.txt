TITLE: ULIP and National Logistics Policy Relationship
SOURCE: Press Information Bureau / Government of India
URL: https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=1860230
DATE: 2022-09-17

The Prime Minister launched the National Logistics Policy on 17 September 2022. The PIB release describes the policy as a driving and guiding force for logistics and states that the National Logistics Policy and GatiShakti are intended to work together to improve India's logistics ecosystem.

The policy launch material emphasizes reducing logistics costs, improving efficiency, ending departmental silos and supporting trade and stakeholders. ULIP is part of the broader digital and data-integration ecosystem supporting logistics.
