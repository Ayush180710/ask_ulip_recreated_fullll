TITLE: ULIP 2023 Progress
SOURCE: Press Information Bureau, Government of India
URL: https://www.pib.gov.in/Pressreleaseshare.aspx?PRID=1990377&lang=2&reg=48
DATE: 2023 Year End Review

The 2023 DPIIT year-end review reported that ULIP had been integrated with 35 systems of eight ministries through 113 APIs and covered more than 1,800 fields. The review also reported 699 industry players registered on ULIP, more than 125 private companies that had signed NDAs, and more than 65 applications live.

The review said GST data was being integrated with ULIP to support end-to-end multimodal tracking of cargo and demand-supply mapping for trade.
