SOURCE: Department for Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade

DATE: accessed for project corpus

The National Logistics Policy (NLP) was launched on 17 September 2022 to complement the PM GatiShakti National Master Plan. The DPIIT overview says PM GatiShakti addresses integrated development of fixed infrastructure and network planning, while NLP addresses soft infrastructure and logistics-sector development including process reforms, improvement in logistics services, digitization, human-resource development and skilling.

The stated aim of NLP 2022 is to develop a technologically enabled, integrated, cost-efficient, resilient, sustainable and trusted logistics ecosystem for accelerated and inclusive growth. Its vision is to drive economic growth and business competitiveness through an integrated, seamless, efficient, reliable, green, sustainable and cost-effective logistics network using technology, processes and skilled manpower.
